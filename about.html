Our Story

Discover the passion and history behind Red Lantern's authentic flavors.

A Legacy of Taste in South Goa

Founded with a deep love for rich, bold flavors, Red Lantern began as a small family kitchen. Today, it stands as a beloved culinary landmark in Colva, blending the delicate art of Chinese cooking with the vibrant, soulful spices of Goan seafood.

Our chefs bring decades of experience to the table, ensuring that every dish—from our sizzling starters to our aromatic curries—is crafted with authenticity and the freshest local ingredients.
