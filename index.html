Welcome to Red Lantern Restaurant

Nestled in the heart of Colva, our cozy open-air garden restaurant offers an unforgettable dining experience under the warm glow of traditional red lanterns.

Whether you're craving authentic Chinese favorites or fresh Goan seafood specialties, our friendly staff ensures every meal is memorable. Perfect for family dinners, romantic evenings, or watching live sports with friends.
